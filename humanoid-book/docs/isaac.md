# NVIDIA Isaac Platform

NVIDIA Isaac ek **advanced robotics simulation platform** hai jo AI-powered robots ke liye design kiya gaya hai.

---

## Isaac Sim

- Photorealistic simulation provide karta hai  
- Synthetic data generate karta hai training ke liye  
- Physics-based robot movement simulate karta hai  

## Isaac ROS

- Hardware-accelerated perception aur control  
- Visual SLAM (VSLAM) aur navigation support  
- ROS 2 ke sath seamless integration  

## Nav2 (Navigation 2)

- Path planning aur bipedal locomotion control ke liye  
- Robot environment samajh ke obstacles avoid karta hai  
- Autonomous movement enable karta hai  

---

### Why NVIDIA Isaac?

- Sim-to-real transfer easy banata hai  
- AI models aur robots ko efficiently train karta hai  
- Bipedal humanoid aur manipulation tasks ke liye ideal
