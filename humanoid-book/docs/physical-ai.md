# What is Physical AI?

Physical AI ka matlab hai **AI jo real duniya mein kaam karta hai**, sirf computer mein nahi.

Digital AI:
- Screen par kaam karta hai  
- Text, images, code samajhta hai  

Physical AI:
- Robots ko control karta hai  
- Real duniya ka physics samajhta hai  
- Cheezein pakarta hai, chal sakta hai, saman utha sakta hai  

## Physical AI ke 3 parts:

### 1. **Brain (AI Model)**
Yeh robot ko batata hai kya karna hai.  
Jaise:
- "Cup pakro"
- "Room clean karo"
- "Door open karo"

### 2. **Body (Robot Hardware)**
Isme hota hai:
- Motors  
- Joints  
- Hands  
- Legs  
- Sensors  

### 3. **Senses (Cameras, Lidar, IMU)**
Robot ko yeh batata hai:
- Samne kya rakha hai  
- Kitna door hai  
- Object kya hai  
- Balance kaise maintain karna hai  

---

## Why is Physical AI important?

Future ke robots:
- Ghar ka kaam karenge  
- Factories mein kaam karenge  
- Hospitals mein help karenge  
- Humans ke sath kaam karenge  

Aur sabse important:

👉 **Humanoid robots human jaise hi honge, isliye easily humans ki jobs assist karenge.**
